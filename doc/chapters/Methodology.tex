\section{Calculations}
Free space wavelength $\lambda_0 = c/f$; c=speed of light.
\[
\lambda_0=\frac{2.99792458\times10^8}{2.3050\times10^9} \cong 0.13006\text{ m}
\]
Guided wavelength $\lambda_g = \frac{\lambda_0}{\sqrt{\epsilon_r}}$. With Rogers RO4350B:
\[
\lambda_g = \frac{0.13006}{\sqrt{3.66}} \cong  35.536\text{ mm}
\]
Patch length:
\[
L = \lambda_g/2 \cong 17.768\text{ mm}
\]
